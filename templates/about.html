WHY THE LEDGER EXISTS

A closer look at a harder-to-see market.

The Private Ledger began on April 28, 2026, to make private-market research accessible. These companies are often difficult to study: information is limited and the important details can be hard to find.

The aim is to understand businesses while they are still private, and follow the trends surrounding their move into public markets.

Research beyond the surface.
